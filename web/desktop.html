Having trouble connecting?

Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi and your phone unlocked. Open the full pairing link in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.

If the page won’t open, check that your Wi-Fi allows devices to talk to each other and your firewall allows the phone.

To allow your phone through the firewall, run this on your computer:

ghostpad-allow-phone PHONE_WIFI_IPV4

The QR and copied link let a phone control this computer. Only share them with your own phone. This connection uses unencrypted HTTP on your local network.
