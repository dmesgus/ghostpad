Gesture guide
Click
Tap anywhere on the main surface. Tap twice to double-click.
Right-click
Tap with two fingers, or tap the bottom-right corner.
Drag & select
Tap, then touch again and slide. Or hold still, then slide. Lift to release.
Scroll & zoom
Slide two fingers or use the side strip to scroll. Flick and lift to coast; touch again to stop. Pinch to zoom.
Move & resize windows
Hold the bottom-left Super zone. Slide one finger on the surface to move a window, or two to resize.
